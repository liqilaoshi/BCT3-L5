对话文本与分句朗读

张经理

Gāo mìshū, huìyì shì cóng liǎng diǎn bàn kāishǐ ma?

高秘书，会议是从两点半开始吗？

Secretary Gao, is the meeting starting at 2:30?

高秘书

Shì de, Zhāng jīnglǐ. Gègè bùmén de fùzérén dōu yǐjīng dào le.

是的，张经理。各个部门的负责人人都已经到了。

Yes, Manager Zhang. Managers from every department have already arrived.

张经理

Hǎo, jīntiān kāihuì yídìng yào bǎ yuányīn jiǎng qīngchu. Xiànzài shì zuì kùnnan de shíhou.

好，今天开会一定要把原因讲清楚。现在是最困难的时候。

OK. Today's meeting must clarify the reasons for the problem. It is the most difficult time.

高秘书

Nín hái yǒu bié de shì ma?

您还有别的事吗？

Is there anything else?

张经理

Méiyǒu le, wǒ xiànzài qù kāihuì. Qǐng bǎ mén guān shàng.

没有了，我现在去开会。请把门关上。

No. I'm going to the meeting. Please close the door.
